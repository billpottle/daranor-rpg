## Visual assets

Many raster assets under `games/*/assets/generated/` and `shared/assets/generated/` were created through AI-assisted image workflows, including OpenAI image-generation tools, followed by human selection and, depending on the asset, cropping, cleanup, compositing, background removal, atlas assembly, resizing, or format conversion.

The `generated` directory name is broader than "AI generated." It also contains procedurally created files, derived display sizes, compressed runtime copies, and hand-processed composites. Legacy generation records are incomplete, so an exact model, model version, or prompt is not available for every existing image.

AI-assisted visual material is offered under CC BY 4.0 only to the extent applicable rights are owned by or licensable by project contributors. No exclusivity or copyrightability warranty is made.

## Requirements for new contributions

For newly contributed AI-assisted media, include the following in the pull request:

- The provider and tool used, plus the model/version when known.
- Which files were generated or materially transformed.
- A short description of human editing and source/reference material.
- The terms or permissions relied upon for submission.
- Any third-party material that remains visible or audible in the result.

Do not submit media unless you have the authority to contribute it under the repository license described in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).
